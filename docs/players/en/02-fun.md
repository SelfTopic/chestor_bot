# 🎲 Games and fun

### wordle (/wordle)

Guess a 5-letter word in 6 tries. The game uses Russian words only, so it isn't available
in your language yet. It will come later.

### quiz (/quiz)

A Tokyo Ghoul lore question with answer buttons. Start it with `/quiz` or the word "quiz".
There are three kinds of questions, picked at random:

- **regular**: a text question and four options;
- **"Who is this?"**: a picture or video and four options;
- **"True or false?"**: a statement (sometimes with a picture) and two buttons. It pays half.

- **Correct answer:** 5,000–10,000 CheSton. Sometimes an illustration comes with the answer.
- Only the one who started the quiz can answer: other people's presses don't count.
- **Streak:** correct answers in a row multiply the reward: ×1, ×1.25, ×1.5 and so on up to
  ×3. Press "Another question" within 5 minutes, or the bot will note under the answer that the
  streak is broken. A wrong answer breaks it too.
- After answering there's an "Another question" button: the new question appears in the same
  message.
- The questions are tricky on purpose: without knowing the lore you'll have to google.
- If the question service is overloaded, the bot tells you how many seconds to wait.

### bot pick

The bot chooses for you. Options go after commas or "or":
`bot pick pizza, sushi or burger`. From 2 to 50 options.

### bot who

The bot answers a question with a random chat member: `bot who is a ghoul today`.
It picks among those who have written in the chat.

### bot random member

Just a random chat member: for giveaways and "who does the dishes".

### bot number

A random number in a range: `bot number 1 100`. The bounds can go in any order.

### calculator (example)

Write an expression and the bot calculates it: `2+2*3`, `(15-3)/4`, `2**10` (two asterisks
is a power). It won't divide by zero and refuses to count huge numbers.

### quote

A random Tokyo Ghoul character quote. The quotes are in Russian only, so the command isn't
available in your language yet. It will come later.
