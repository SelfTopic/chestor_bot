# 💍 Family and marriage

Marriage here is a **family**: it can have any number of people, and everyone is married to
everyone else. Two husbands and two wives, five friends, whatever you like. A family is one for
the whole bot, in every chat.

- You can be in only **one** family.
- A family has a **shared account**: its members have no personal money. When you join, you give
  the family all your CheSton, and from then on all earnings and spending go through the family
  account.
- Spouses don't fight each other.

| What | Price |
|:--|--:|
| Proposal | 250,000 CheSton, paid by the one who invites |
| Consent | 250,000 CheSton, paid by the newcomer when the marriage happens |
| Divorce | free, but you leave with nothing |

### marry (wedding)

Invite someone to your family, or start a family of two if you're single.

- **How:** "marry" or "wedding" as a reply to the person's message, or `marry @username`,
  `marry <id>`, `marry` with a clickable name.
- You can only invite a **single** person: if they're already in a family, they have to divorce
  first.
- The bot asks whether you really want to pay **250,000** CheSton. Confirm and the money is
  taken, and an invitation with buttons appears.
- The marriage happens when **everyone** agrees: you ("I confirm" button), the newcomer
  ("I agree") and every member of your family. The family confirms in a private chat with the
  bot, via the button under the invitation.
- When the marriage happens, the newcomer pays another **250,000** and gives the family the rest
  of their money.
- **Refusal.** The newcomer can press "Decline", a family member "Against" in the private
  chat. One refusal is enough: no wedding.
- **The term is 1 hour.** If not everyone agrees within an hour, there's no wedding.
- Both on refusal and when the hour is up, the 250,000 for the proposal is gone: no refunds.

### family

Your family: who's in it, since when and how long already (e.g. "1mo 10d"), and how much is in
the shared account.

### families

All families of this chat: a family is listed if at least one of its members is in the chat.
It shows the family name (if any), all its members, since when it exists and how long it's been.

### family name

Name your family: `family name Anteiku`. Free, up to **32 characters**, different families can
have the same name.

- The name changes only if **all** family members agree: "I agree" and "Against" buttons under
  the message, and those not in this chat use the "Answer in private chat" button.
- One "against" is enough to reject the name. The term is **1 hour**.
- While the family is deciding on one name, you can't propose another.
- The name shows in **family**, **families** and **top bal** instead of the member list.

### divorce

Leave the family. Free, but **without money**: everything stays in the family account.

- The bot asks again: confirm with the "Divorce" button.
- If only one person is left in the family, the family falls apart, and its whole account
  becomes their personal money.
