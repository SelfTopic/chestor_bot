# 👋 Getting started

CheStor is a Tokyo Ghoul bot and a chat helper at the same time: a game, moderation, videos
and music from links. Almost every command is written **as plain text without a slash**,
exactly as in the subsection headings.

## First steps

1. Send the bot `/start` in a private chat: without it, the bot can't send you duel buttons
   and reminders.
2. Subscribe to the [@chestor_channel](https://t.me/chestor_channel) channel: the game won't
   open without a subscription.
3. **grow kagune** to become a ghoul.
4. **snap** for your first money.
5. **race profile** to see what you've become.

### /start

Meet the bot. Most importantly, it opens a private chat between you and the bot: Telegram
doesn't let bots write first. Without it you won't get duel choice buttons, hunger reminders
or your obituary.

### channel subscription

Only subscribers of [@chestor_channel](https://t.me/chestor_channel) can play as a ghoul (all
commands from "Ghoul and kagune", fights, coffee, snap and the rest). The channel has bot news,
events and giveaways. Without a subscription the bot shows two buttons instead of the game:
"Subscribe" and "I subscribed". Subscribe, press the second one and play. Everything else
(profile, music, videos, moderation) works without a subscription.

### language (/lang)

The language the bot speaks: buttons with languages. In a group only admins can change it, and
it's one for the whole chat. In a private chat everyone picks their own. Until you pick one, the
bot speaks your Telegram language, and in a new chat the language of whoever added it.

### profile (/profile)

Your card: name, race (human or ghoul), CheSton balance. The ghoul profile with stats is
**race profile**.

### /help

Help right inside the bot.

- `/help` lists the sections with links to the docs channel.
- `/help <command>` shows one command or mechanic in detail: `/help snap`, `/help duel`,
  `/help coffee`, `/help hunger`.

It searches by command name, a part of it works too: `/help coffee` finds **drink coffee**.

### bot (chestor)

Just write **bot** and the bot answers.
