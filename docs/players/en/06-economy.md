# 💰 Money: CheSton

| How to earn | How much | How often |
|:--|--:|:--|
| **snap** | 500–1,500 | once every 10 minutes |
| **drink coffee** | 5,000–9,000 | once every 30 minutes, after 100 snaps |
| **/quiz** | 5,000–10,000, up to ×3 for a streak | per correct answer |
| **fight mob** | depends on level | once every 10 minutes |
| new level | more the higher the level | — |

### cult hashtags

Wear `#рабчестора` or `#честормойпапа` in your Telegram name and get **10,000 CheSton** every
hour, as long as you've used the bot at least once in the last day. The bot tells you in a
private chat; the button under the message turns the notifications off, and the CheSton keep
coming.

### bal

Your CheSton balance. If you're in a family, it's the shared family account: family members
have no personal money.

### top bal

The richest: `top bal` shows the first 20, `top bal 50` up to 50 places. A family takes one
line with its shared account.

### snap

Break your fingers like Kaneki and get CheSton.

- **Reward:** 500–1,500 CheSton.
- **Cooldown:** 10 minutes. Too early and the bot tells you how long to wait.
- The bot counts broken fingers: 100 of them unlock **drink coffee**, and there's **top snap**.

### drink coffee

Coffee from Anteiku, the most generous way to earn.

- **Reward:** 5,000–9,000 CheSton.
- **Unlocks** after 100 broken fingers (**snap**).
- **Cooldown:** 30 minutes.

> ⚠️ **Don't drink too early.** Trying to drink coffee on cooldown is an overdose: the bot
> takes one reward and closes coffee **for a day**.

### send / pay (/transfer)

Send CheSton to another player.

- **As a reply** to the receiver's message: `send 500`.
- **By name** in any chat: `send @username 500` or `send <id> 500`.
- All three are the same: **send**, **pay**, **/transfer**.

A transfer is confirmed **twice**: out of four buttons only "confirm" is right, the others
cancel the transfer. That way you won't give money away by accident.

**Limits:**

- from 1 to 100,000 CheSton at once;
- you can send once you've been in the bot for at least **3 days**;
- one receiver accepts no more than **10 transfers a day**;
- you can't send to yourself or your spouse: the family already shares an account.

A family member sends from the family account, and transfers to them land there too.

### bet (lottery)

A bet on a color: **bet <color> <amount>**, e.g. `bet red 500`.

- A bet is from 100 to 100,000 CheSton, the money is taken right away.
- The bot spins a wheel (a video), the result comes when it stops.

| Color | Payout | Chance |
|:--|:--:|:--:|
| 🔴 red | ×1.8 | 28% |
| 🔵 blue | ×2.5 | 22% |
| 🟢 green | ×3 | 20% |
| 🟡 yellow | ×5 | 13% |
| ⚪ white | ×10 | 10% |

> On average the lottery returns less than you bet (white returns exactly as much): it's fun,
> not a way to earn.
